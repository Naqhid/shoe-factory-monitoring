# 🎓 ProdPulse Training Guide
## Role-Based Training Program

---

## 📋 **Training Overview**

This guide outlines specific training content for each user role in ProdPulse. Each role has distinct responsibilities and access levels.

### **Training Duration by Role:**
- **Production Workers**: 2-3 hours
- **Quality Controllers**: 3-4 hours  
- **Supervisors/Line Managers**: 5-6 hours
- **Plant Managers**: 4-5 hours
- **System Administrators**: 6-8 hours
- **IT Support Staff**: 6-8 hours

---

# 👷♂️ **PRODUCTION WORKERS TRAINING**
*Mobile users who track their own attendance and production*

## **🎯 What Production Workers Need to Learn:**

### **Core Responsibilities:**
- ✅ Use mobile app for personal attendance
- ✅ Scan employee QR codes (their own only)
- ✅ Update personal work session status
- ✅ View basic personal performance metrics

### **📚 Training Modules:**

#### **Module 1: Mobile App Basics (45 minutes)**
**What to Teach:**
- How to access ProdPulse on mobile device
- Installing the mobile app (PWA)
- Login with personal credentials
- Basic mobile navigation
- Understanding personal dashboard

**Hands-on Practice:**
- Install app on personal device
- Login and logout
- Navigate personal screens
- Practice touch gestures

#### **Module 2: Employee QR Code Scanning (30 minutes)**
**What to Teach:**
- What their employee QR code represents
- How to scan their own QR code for attendance
- Check-in and check-out process
- What information appears after scanning
- Troubleshooting scanning issues

**Hands-on Practice:**
- Scan personal employee QR code
- Practice attendance check-in/out
- Handle scan failures
- Practice with different lighting conditions

#### **Module 3: Personal Production Tracking (45 minutes)**
**What to Teach:**
- How to start/end personal work sessions
- Recording completed work
- Updating personal production status
- Understanding personal performance metrics

**Hands-on Practice:**
- Clock in/out using mobile app
- Update personal production numbers
- Record work completion
- View personal performance data

#### **Module 4: Basic Support (15 minutes)**
**What to Teach:**
- What to do if app doesn't work
- Who to contact for help (supervisor/IT)
- Basic connectivity checks
- When to ask for assistance

**Assessment Criteria:**
- ✅ Can login independently
- ✅ Can scan own employee QR code for attendance
- ✅ Can update personal production status
- ✅ Knows who to contact for help

#### **Module 5: Operational Reporting (60 minutes)**
**What to Teach:**
- Generating supervisor-level reports
- Including QR scan data in reports
- Customizing report parameters
- Scheduling automated reports
- Sharing reports with upper management

**Hands-on Practice:**
- Generate efficiency and productivity reports
- Include machine and employee scan data
- Customize date ranges and filters
- Export reports in different formats

#### **Module 6: Decision Making (45 minutes)**
**What to Teach:**
- Using real-time data for operational decisions
- When to intervene in production processes
- Using QR scan insights for quick decisions
- Escalation procedures to management
- Communication protocols

**Assessment Criteria:**
- ✅ Can scan both machine and employee QR codes effectively
- ✅ Can monitor team and machine performance
